# Keep writing

- **Title:** Keep writing
- **Command:** `keep-writing`
- **ChatGPT Canvas equivalent:** asking the model to continue the text

Before sending, adjust the amount in brackets if you like.

**Content** (paste into the prompt's content field):

```text
Keep writing from the end of the note (or right after the passage I selected) in the same voice, tone, tense and point of view, for about [3 paragraphs]. Use insert_text and do not change anything already written.
```
